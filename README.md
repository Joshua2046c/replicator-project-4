A modular desktop organizer with two removable inserts, 11 compartments, and a shallow front tray for pens, stationery, and small essentials. Its stepped profile, sloped openings, and rounded transitions keep items visible and within easy reach. Designed for 3D printing in three separate pieces, it assembles without screws or glue, while the lift-out inserts make cleaning easy and allow contrasting colors for a personal touch.
